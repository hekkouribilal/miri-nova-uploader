Miri Nova Uploader

A private desktop client used by the owner of the Miri Life YouTube channel to upload completed episodes, metadata, thumbnails, and captions through the official YouTube Data API v3.

Private tool: this client is not offered to the public and does not operate on other users' channels.

What it does

The owner prepares and validates an upload package, authorizes the Miri Life channel with Google OAuth 2.0, and initiates each upload. The client uses the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl scopes.

Data handling

OAuth credentials and upload records remain on the owner's computer. The client has no external database, advertising service, or analytics service.
