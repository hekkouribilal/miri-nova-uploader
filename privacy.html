Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Miri Nova Uploader is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the Miri Life YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not provide accounts or services to third-party users.

Information the tool accesses

With the channel owner's authorization through Google OAuth 2.0, the tool accesses only the YouTube information and files needed to:

  • identify the authorized YouTube channel;
  • upload videos and set titles, descriptions, tags, category, language, audience, disclosure, privacy, and scheduling settings;
  • upload custom thumbnails and caption files; and
  • receive the resulting YouTube video and API response identifiers.

The tool requests the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl OAuth scopes. It does not read contacts, email, payment information, passwords, or unrelated Google Account data.

How information is used

Information is used only to upload and manage content for the owner's Miri Life YouTube channel, confirm that the correct channel is authorized, prevent accidental duplicate uploads, and diagnose upload failures.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer in a private configuration directory. Upload records are also stored locally and contain technical details needed to prevent duplicate uploads. Miri Nova Uploader has no external database, analytics service, advertising system, or server-side storage.

Local OAuth tokens are retained until the owner revokes access or deletes the local token file. Local upload records are retained until the owner deletes them.

Sharing and sale of information

The tool sends authorized video files, metadata, thumbnails, captions, and OAuth requests directly to Google and YouTube only as necessary to perform the requested upload. Information is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or other third parties. Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Security

OAuth secrets and tokens are stored outside the project source code with owner-only file permissions. The tool uses HTTPS and Google's official OAuth 2.0 and YouTube Data API endpoints. It does not ask the owner to enter a Google password into the tool.

User control and deletion

The owner can revoke the tool's Google Account access at any time from Google Account third-party connections. The owner can also delete the local token and upload-log files from the computer.

Children

This private uploader is not directed to children and does not collect information from child users. Any audience designation applied to a YouTube video is set explicitly by the channel owner when preparing that upload.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Miri Life YouTube channel.
